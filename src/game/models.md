# Model information

## Mismatch

Arundel, Bodiam, and Camber all have a mismatch between the encoder used in training, and that used here. They are kept for historical comparison.

Their corresponding corrections are Farnham, Gidleigh, and Hastings respectively.

Bodiam and Camber (and therefore Farnham and Gidleigh) both have flaws with what data the encoder is supposed to be representing, but this was present in training also.
